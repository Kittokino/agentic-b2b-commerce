# The Olive Tree × FreshSupply: story

> Concept for a Team Lead Research & Design case. All companies, people and figures are fictional.

## The principle
Use only as many actors and use cases as it takes to cover the brief. Anything else is parked.

## Places
| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Olive Tree** | A restaurant chain: a head office (Priya) and 3 outlets: **Harbour** (Nigel's kitchen), Old Town, Riverside |
| **FreshSupply** | A food & beverage wholesaler running on Sana: warehouse, service desk (Tom), commerce admin (Ravi) |
| **The road** | FreshSupply's logistics partner delivers to the outlets. In the background only |
| **Sana** | The platform under FreshSupply; it serves many wholesalers in many industries |

## Cast (5)
| Actor | Side | Why they're in the cast | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Priya**, central buyer | The Olive Tree | The decision-maker; keeps the user in control | Desktop portal, email, WhatsApp, calls |
| **Chef Nigel**, head chef, Harbour only | The Olive Tree | The moment with no screen; where demand starts. Orders day to day within Priya's rules | WhatsApp voice notes and text |
| **FreshSupply agent** | FreshSupply | Acts for FreshSupply, and knows when to stop | WhatsApp, portal, email |
| **Tom**, service rep | FreshSupply | The hand-over to a person | Sana service desk, phone |
| **Ravi**, commerce admin | FreshSupply | The platform: how a merchant configures the agent | Sana admin |

**Parked actors:** Lina (outlet manager), the owner, Maria (account manager), the driver. **Priya's own AI assistant** is a single talking point about the future (buyers will bring their own agents, so merchants must be readable by agents), not an actor.

## Two rules
1. **Spend limit:** Harbour orders over **€1,500** need Priya's OK. (Priya sets this limit at FreshSupply; FreshSupply only sees its own share of her spend.)
2. **Substitution:** a substitute that changes what reaches the end customer (here: a dish on a printed event menu) or costs more than the contract price needs the buyer's consent.

## The journey (UC1 → UC2, one order)
1. **Wed 22:47 · WhatsApp voice note.** Nigel: *"Private dinner Saturday, 120 covers. The usual event set, plus about 18 kilos of salmon."*
2. **The agent reads the order back** (~€2,200). Over €1,500 → **stop 1:** it sends the order to Priya instead of refusing Nigel.
3. **Thu 07:30 · portal.** Priya approves.
4. **Salmon is 10 kg short.** Trout would change the dish on the printed menu and costs more than contract → **stop 2:** the agent explains the options and asks.
5. **Priya asks for a person → Tom** picks up the case with full context and finds salmon at a partner depot.
6. **One confirmation, one order,** shown in WhatsApp (Nigel), the portal and email (Priya).

**Then, behind the scenes:** Ravi's admin view shows the two rules that fired, and the same rules set up for a **medical supplier** (substitution needs a clinician's approval).

## Parked use cases
Delivery delay with impact on service · damaged crate and credit note · waste insight · month-end summary · spend drifting to a competitor.

## What to remember on the day
**5 actors · 1 order · 2 rules · 2 stopping points · 3 channels · 2 industries.**
