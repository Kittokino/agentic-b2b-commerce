Agentic B2B commerce

A design exploration by Supreo

Instructions to view the presentation

View it on a laptop or desktop, in full screen. It has two parts, a deck, which takes about 10 minutes, and a prototype, which takes about 15. Slide 9 of the deck opens the prototype, so you can start with the deck and follow it from there.

1. The deck

kittokino.github.io/agentic-b2b-commerce/deck

The challenge — guiding principles — the places and the people — a day in their lives — the situation — the agent's decisions — the journey — the platform — the design system — the vision.

Move with ← → or the buttons at the bottom right. Press N for the speaker notes, which tell the story behind each slide.

2. The prototype

kittokino.github.io/agentic-b2b-commerce/prototype

Each scene has three parts. On the left, a column tells the story: when it happens, whose screen you are looking at, and what happens next. On the right is that person's own screen, such as Nigel's WhatsApp, Priya's Teams or Kade's activity in Sana. The controls run along the bottom.

You can move through it in two ways:

  • Act as the person. Click the highlighted part of a screen, such as a button in a message, and the story moves on. Now and then, the button at the bottom right switches to the next person's screen.
  • Step through it. Use the arrows at the bottom to go through every scene to the end.

At the end, "Back to the deck" returns you to the deck.

All companies, people and figures are fictional.
