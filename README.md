# Agentic B2B commerce

A design exploration of how agentic AI changes B2B buying and selling. It follows one order at a food and beverage wholesaler, **Kade**, and its customer, the restaurant chain **The Olive Tree**: from a chef's voice note on WhatsApp to a confirmed delivery, with an AI order agent that acts within rules, stops to ask, and hands over to a person.

All companies, people and figures are fictional.
